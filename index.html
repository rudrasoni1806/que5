Rhythm & Radiance 2026

One campus. Many talents. Unforgettable memories.

Events

Get ready for an exciting celebration of creativity, teamwork and talent at Rhythm & Radiance 2026. Students can take part in music, dance, drama, photography and fun competitions throughout the fest.

From energetic stage performances to interactive games, every event is planned to give participants a chance to learn new skills, meet friends and enjoy the vibrant spirit of college life.

Guests

Our college fest will welcome inspiring guest speakers, experienced artists and special performers from different creative fields. Their stories and performances will encourage students to explore their interests and aim high.

Attendees will also have opportunities to interact with invited guests, hear about their journeys and take away fresh ideas to support their academic and creative goals.

Contact

For registration details, event schedules or volunteering opportunities, contact the student fest committee through the college activities office. Our team will be happy to guide participants and answer general questions.
